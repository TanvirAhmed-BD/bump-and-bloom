Pregnancy Week 12: The End of the First Trimester

You made it! Your baby now has reflexes, fingers that open and close, and kidneys that work — and for many moms, the nausea finally starts to lift. Here's your complete guide to week 12.

Your baby this week

Your baby is the size of a lime — and the reflexes have switched on: if you poke your belly, your baby likely squirms in response (you won't feel it yet). Fingers open and close, toes curl, the baby makes sucking motions, and the kidneys produce urine that's released into the amniotic fluid.

The digestive system is practicing its contractions, and the bone marrow is making white blood cells. Almost every organ and system is now formed — from here, the work is maturing and growing.

Your body & symptoms: the tide turns

As the placenta fully takes over hormone production, many moms notice nausea loosening, energy returning in pockets, and food becoming appealing again. Your uterus has risen above your pubic bone — your doctor may now find the heartbeat with a handheld Doppler at appointments, and a small bump may start hinting itself.

If your symptoms haven't eased yet, hang on: for many women the turn comes in the next week or two.

The NT scan & first-trimester screening

If you chose screening, your NT ultrasound (weeks 11–13+) and blood work likely happen around now. The results give a personalized risk estimate for certain chromosomal conditions. Whatever you decide about screening is a personal choice — your doctor's job is clear information, not pressure.

Sharing the news

Many parents treat week 12 as the green light to announce widely — the end of the first trimester is a natural milestone. Tell people in whatever order and way feels right to you; there's no rulebook.

⚠️ Warning signs — call your doctor the same day

Heavy bleeding · severe pain · fainting · fever. They still matter — if something feels wrong, making the call is always the right move.

✅ Mama checklist — Week 12

  • Screening done or booked — the NT window is weeks 11–13
  • Celebrate the milestone — you've earned it
  • Look ahead: the anatomy scan comes around week 20
  • If energy is returning, gently rebuild routines you enjoy
  • Share the news whenever and however feels right — there's no rulebook

Key takeaways

  • Your baby has reflexes, working kidneys and nearly every system formed
  • For many moms symptoms start lifting as the placenta takes over
  • NT screening happens in the 11–13 week window if you chose it
  • Week 12 is the natural milestone many parents use to share their news
